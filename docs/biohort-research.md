## Live current configurator findings (direct UI observation)

Roomle embedded in Biohort page. Four steps: Sizes & Colours, Door, Foundation, Accessories. Current size selection is a dropdown containing nine sizes. Two colors. Model defaults to 3 × 4 m, Metallic dark grey. External page presents Back to product, Print/PDF/CSV, Share, Search dealers, Contact; no price visible in the other-country version inspected. Roomle has AR, multi-select, measurements, view selector, fullscreen, camera reset, snapshot. Landing promises saved configurations for six months.

Door variations: additional door; second door wing; glass additional door; glass double door panel; glass door insert. Foundation step offers floor frame with/without. Accessories have cryptic names and catalog-style images: roof support coating, double acrylic glazing, rainwater pipes front/back, shelves, hooks, electrical panels, BikeMax, folding table, sauna module, wall glass element, tilt-turn window, external-light preparation.

UX opportunities inferred: communicate purposeful steps/progress; distinguish nominal size from usable area; prevent impossible size combinations; demonstrate benefits rather than technical labels; retain model/summary through form; gate a clear estimate + useful PDF package with small transparent form; make CTA singular and persistent.

## Product boundaries and dimensions

Source: current product technical data. Exterior finish **Metallic dark grey** or **Metallic quartz grey** only. Interior grey white. Colors are proprietary and have no matching RAL number. Wall insulation is 40 mm. Roof insulation with XPS is an additional customer-installed layer. Standard roof is plywood plus EPDM. Standard opening skylight circles the room. Product source explicitly advises checking local planning rules.

Dimensions in metres; internal sizes exclude supports. Height overall **2.505 m**; interior height **2.29 m** for width 3, **2.24 m** for width 4.

Compatibility: glass door insert requires standard/additional door right hinge and second double-door wing left hinge. Double acrylic skylight sacrifices ventilation, cannot retrofit. Floor frame cannot retrofit; required for aluminium floor panel or point foundation. Folding table and regular shelves/hook set not compatible with depth2 (corner variants exist). Full-height glass wall elements can sit adjacent subject to wall length. Lighting price is cutout preparation, **does not include spotlights**. Electrical mounting panel does **not** mean supplied electrics.

## Trust and brand

Official logo: green lowercase biohort with blue roof/mountain line. White UI, olive green CTAs, restrained blue accent, warm grey backgrounds; product photography rather than wood-clad room visuals. Made in Austria. 20-year guarantee subject to terms. Current guarantee requires professional assembly/use/maintenance and limits guaranteed use to garden equipment/furniture/bicycles etc. Link terms next to the promise; do not imply unrestricted residential use.
