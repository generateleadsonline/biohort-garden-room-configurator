# Biohort source conflicts (agent B: Neo, Panorama, CasaNova)

Checked 13 Oct 2026. Every disagreement found between the GB product page (JSON-LD offers, data sheet, page text, embedded "Matching accessories"), the GB price lists and the brochures. Values are quoted as published; nothing has been corrected.

Sources:

- Neo page: https://www.biohort.com/gb-en/p/garden-shed-neo?country_id=gb
- Panorama page: https://www.biohort.com/gb-en/p/garden-shed-panorama?country_id=gb
- CasaNova page: https://www.biohort.com/gb-en/p/casanova?country_id=gb
- GB price list 2026 No. 71 gb, Version 02 (all ranges except CasaNova): https://www.biohort.com/asset/Inhalte/Allgemeine-Seiten/Preislisten/Endkundenpreisliste_gb.pdf

### N2. 3B foundation 229 x 217 cm

Both 3B rows (standard and double door) give foundation 229 x 217 cm. ### N6. SmartBase 1D/4A width

SmartBase 1D, 4A (43956): 344 x 166 x 10 cm. SmartBasePLUS 1D, 4A: 334 x 166 x 65; floor frame 1D, 4A: 334 x 166 x 4; the other D-width SmartBase sizes are 334. Source: https://www.biohort.com/asset/Inhalte/Allgemeine-Seiten/Preislisten/Endkundenpreisliste_gb.pdf p15. Probably a typo for 334.

### N7. Side canopy 3A-3D in metallic silver missing from web accessories

The price list offers Side Canopy 3A, 3B, 3C, 3D in metallic silver, SKU 81036, at GBP 3,009 (https://www.biohort.com/asset/Inhalte/Allgemeine-Seiten/Preislisten/Endkundenpreisliste_gb.pdf p16). The Neo page's embedded "Matching accessories" data has 81026 and 81046 (silver 2x and 4x) and all quartz grey and dark grey canopies, but no 81036 (https://www.biohort.com/gb-en/p/garden-shed-neo?country_id=gb). Quartz grey 2A-2D (87026) appears twice as often as the other canopy SKUs, so the silver 3x bundle slot may point at the wrong SKU. Confirm before showing a silver 3x canopy.

### N8. Shelf basket width

Shelf Baskets 46026 are listed among the Neo grey-white 56 cm interior accessories, but measure 72 x 16 x 11 cm, the HighLine/Panorama shelf width (https://www.biohort.com/asset/Inhalte/Allgemeine-Seiten/Preislisten/Endkundenpreisliste_gb.pdf p17, marked "galvanised only"). The web lists the same SKU as "Shelf baskets Neo" (https://www.biohort.com/gb-en/p/garden-shed-neo?country_id=gb). Check that it fits Neo support rails.

### N9. Delivery wording

Web: "Free Shipping within 3 weeks"; FAQ: delivered within 3 weeks of order confirmation (https://www.biohort.com/gb-en/p/garden-shed-neo?country_id=gb). Price list: "Stock items ready for dispatch within 2 to 4 working days after order clarification" (https://www.biohort.com/asset/Inhalte/Allgemeine-Seiten/Preislisten/Endkundenpreisliste_gb.pdf p1). Product Variety brochure: "Dispatched within 2 to 4 days" and "Free Delivery* ... *applies to stock items, excl. CasaNova, CasaVita and other oversized products" (https://www.biohort.com/asset/downloads/files/Prospekte/Minifolder_EN.pdf p18). These are dispatch and delivery times, not necessarily a contradiction.

### N10. Free-kerbside country lists differ inside the same FAQ

The first list includes Czech Republic and Slovenia but not Norway. The second includes Norway but not Czech Republic or Slovenia. Both include Great Britain (https://www.biohort.com/gb-en/p/garden-shed-neo?country_id=gb, FAQ).
