A few things you might be wondering.

Can I choose any size?

CasaNova has nine compatible modular sizes. The sliders snap to those sizes, so every combination you see is available. A 4 × 3 m model is not part of the range. Footprint and usable internal area are shown separately.

What will my estimate include?

Your itemised estimate uses Biohort’s published 2026 UK price list, including 20% VAT and transport to the dealer’s warehouse. Your chosen accessories are included. Home delivery, assembly, foundations, electrics, roof insulation, permissions and site works require separate confirmation. It is a budgeting guide, subject to dealer confirmation.

Is CasaNova suitable as a year-round office?

CasaNova is a designer outbuilding with 40 mm insulated walls. Heating, ventilation, roof and floor insulation, electrics and suitability for your intended use need specialist advice. These are not included as a fully finished office package.

What happens when I request my quote?

Submit your name, email and project postcode to unlock your estimate and download your personalised PDF immediately. Your design and enquiry are securely saved for this demonstration. No automated email or sales follow-up is sent. Optional marketing consent is separate.
